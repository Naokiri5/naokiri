## Immediate priorities from this range

1. Preserve existing strict paper executable-depth/incident safeguards; do not replace them with ticker fallback or a duplicate governor.
2. Establish evidence for concurrent capital reservations, full lifecycle recovery and immutable actual fill history before unattended certification.
3. Treat news provenance/future dates and unqualified release-evidence claims as correctness work, not cosmetic fixes.
4. Reuse integrated DITAS/Twin/worker accounting/Academy services instead of rebuilding them from ticket sketches.
5. Keep real funding, signing and withdrawals design-only/blocked; no external scope, hosting, backup restore or 24-hour soak has been established by this document.
